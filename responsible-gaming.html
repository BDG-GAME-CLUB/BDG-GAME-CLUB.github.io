TIME

Use an alarm, take breaks and never extend a session to chase an outcome.

MONEY

Set a hard limit

Use only disposable entertainment money. A limit should never move upward mid-session.

Colour prediction facts

Past colours, streaks, influencers and paid tip groups do not guarantee future outcomes. Treat every claim of a “100% winning trick” or fixed return as a red flag.

Warning signs to take seriously

  • Spending more time or money than planned.
  • Borrowing, hiding activity or missing bills.
  • Trying to recover losses immediately.
  • Feeling anxious, angry or unable to stop.
  • Letting play affect work, sleep or relationships.
If any warning sign feels familiar, stop play, block access where possible and speak with someone you trust or a qualified local support service.

Getting help

Use reputable support services in your country, financial blocking tools offered by your bank and self-exclusion options provided by the service. In an immediate crisis or danger, contact local emergency services.
