Set limits before
the first round.

A useful limit is specific, visible and decided while calm. It does not move because a session is winning or losing.

Build your planMoney limitTime limitBreaksStop signs

Choose a hard money limit

Start with an entertainment amount that can be lost without affecting food, rent, bills, debt or savings. Keep it separate from essential money. Never borrow to play.

Set a start and stop time

Decide the end time before signing in and use an alarm outside the game. Fast rounds can hide how long a session has lasted.

Plan breaks, not just an ending

Step away regularly, move around and check how you feel. A break is especially important after a strong win, a loss or an urge to increase stakes.

Know the signs to stop completely

  • You have reached either limit.
  • You want to chase a loss immediately.
  • You are hiding play or borrowing money.
  • You feel angry, anxious or unable to focus.
  • Sleep, work or relationships are being affected.
When stopping feels difficult, use self-exclusion or blocking tools, contact your bank about gambling blocks and speak with a qualified local support service.

A simple written promise

“I will spend no more than ___, stop at ___, take a break every ___ minutes, and leave immediately if play stops feeling like entertainment.”
